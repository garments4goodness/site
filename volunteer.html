Volunteer & Leadership

Help lead our next stage.

We are looking for students and volunteers who want to build chapters, grow outreach, create content, and tell the stories behind our work.

Open Roles

Choose the leadership path that matches how you want to contribute.

Digital

Social Media Team Member

Help manage TikTok, Instagram, and SEO projects so more people can discover and support the mission.

Best for: creators, editors, designers, and strategy-minded volunteers.

Local Leadership

Chapter Lead

Start a Garments4Goodness chapter in your area and organize donation drives, outreach, and local action.

Best for: students ready to build a team in their community.

Operations

Chapter Coordination Lead

Support new chapters as they get started and help chapter leaders stay connected to organization-wide resources.

Best for: organized communicators who like helping teams succeed.

Outreach

Outreach Team Member

Help build partnerships, research grants, and connect Garments4Goodness with schools, businesses, and community groups.

Best for: persuasive writers and relationship-builders.

Stories

Lead Reporter

Write articles and updates for the website that highlight chapter work, drives, partnerships, and community impact.

Best for: writers who care about clear, human-centered storytelling.
